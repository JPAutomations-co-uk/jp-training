When the base is there, everything accelerates.

When your training, nutrition, and sleep are locked in, every booster we add actually does what it's supposed to. They're not shortcuts. They're accelerants on a fire that's already burning.

Targeted Supplementation

Not a pre-packaged stack. Not what worked for someone in a YouTube comment section. The supplements worth taking, at the right doses, for the specific gaps in your biology — nothing else.

Breathing & Nervous System

Most men breathe wrong and have no idea it's affecting their recovery, stress response, and focus. CO2 tolerance work, HRV biofeedback, NSDR — the inputs that regulate the system everything else depends on.

Cold & Heat Exposure

Cold exposure, done consistently, does more than spike adrenaline — it trains your nervous system to stop catastrophising under discomfort. Sauna has real cardiovascular and longevity data behind it. Both work. Both are routinely done wrong.

Circadian Optimisation

Your body runs on a 24-hour biological clock. Morning light, meal timing, temperature, screen exposure — every one of these either works with it or against it. Most men fight it without realising. Fixing it costs nothing and the impact is immediate.

Advanced Sleep Protocols

Seven hours that actually restore you is a very different thing from seven hours that just pass. Temperature, light, supplement timing, sleep architecture — the difference shows up in your testosterone, your focus, and how you feel at 2pm.

VO2 Max & Cardiovascular

VO2 max predicts how long you live more accurately than cholesterol, blood pressure, or BMI. Men in the lowest quintile have five times the mortality risk of those in the highest. Most men have no idea where they sit. It's trainable — significantly, and fast.

Stage Three

COMPLETE
OPTIMISATION

When lifestyle isn't enough.

You can do everything right — train well, eat well, sleep well — and still be operating at 70% because of something invisible. A hormonal imbalance. An inflammatory marker. A thyroid issue your GP marked as normal because it was technically inside range. At this stage, we measure everything.

Not because data is exciting. Because without it, you're guessing. And guessing is what most men do — not for a month, but for the rest of their lives.

Blood Markers Analysed
Testosterone (total + free), SHBG, cortisol, full thyroid, ApoB, insulin, Vitamin D, hs-CRP — every marker that tells the real story

Body Composition Scan
Visceral fat mass, lean muscle by region, bone density. Not scales — precision tissue mapping that tells the hormonal story BMI never could

Complete Hormone Panel

Max + Lactate Threshold
Exact zone prescription for Zone 2, threshold pace, sprint capacity. The fitness number most predictive of how long you live — measured precisely

CGM

4 weeks of real-time glucose response to every meal, drink, stress event, and night of poor sleep. The most eye-opening data most men have ever seen about their bodies
DNA
Genetic Testing
MTHFR, APOE, COMT, FADS1 — how your genetics shape your methylation, Alzheimer's risk, dopamine clearance, and omega-3 conversion. Protocol adjusted accordingly
AGE
Biological Age (DunedinPACE)
Your biological age versus your chronological age. The single most important number in longevity — measured, tracked, and moved in the right direction
