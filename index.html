You can do everything right — train well, eat well, sleep well — and still be operating at 70% because of something invisible. A hormonal imbalance. An inflammatory marker. A thyroid issue your GP marked as normal because it was technically inside range. At this stage, we measure everything.

Not because data is exciting. Because without it, you're guessing. And guessing is what most men do — not for a month, but for the rest of their lives.

Exact zone prescription for Zone 2, threshold pace, sprint capacity. The fitness number most predictive of how long you live — measured precisely

CGM

4 weeks of real-time glucose response to every meal, drink, stress event, and night of poor sleep. The most eye-opening data most men have ever seen about their bodies

AGE

Your biological age versus your chronological age. The single most important number in longevity — measured, tracked, and moved in the right direction
